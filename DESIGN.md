## Brand & Style

This design system expresses executive leadership, fiscal rigor, strategic precision, and institutional stability. Tailored specifically for C-suite recruiters, board members, and executive committees reviewing top-tier profiles in Financial Controllership and FP&A, the aesthetic balances modern corporate minimalism with bespoke editorial authority.

The visual style merges structured corporate modernism with high-end editorial finance elements:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, measured, analytical, and uncompromisingly professional.
- **Visual Personality:** Crisp typography, sharp data delineation, generous asymmetric breathing room, and controlled luxury through subtle warm amber accents.
- **Impact:** Communicates stewardship of capital, clarity under complex financial modeling, and proven governance from the first glance.

## Colors

The palette leverages a deep navy foundation contrasted against crisp architectural surfaces, accented by precision petrol blue and measured gold highlights.

- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate / Navy):** Dominates high-level structural hierarchy, headers, primary buttons, and critical framing. Evokes stability, institutional depth, and governance.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` - Petrol Blue):** Applied to active states, contextual links, progressive data bars, and analytical interactive elements. Represents forward planning and strategic execution.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Amber / Executive Gold):** Used sparingly for high-value metric callouts, verified certifications, key business impact numbers, and milestone badges.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` - Off-White Canvas):** Clean, glare-free canvas paired with borders in `#E2E8F0` and muted body text in `#334155` to ensure effortless legibility during detailed reviews.
- **Success & Metrics (`#059669`):** Reserved for positive financial delta badges (+EBITDA, ROI gains, cost reduction percentages).

## Typography

The typographical pairing pairs editorial gravitas with analytical precision:

- **Headlines & Section Anchors (`Merriweather`):** Evokes legacy financial publications and board dossiers. Used for candidate name, executive summary introductions, and macro career phase titles.
- **Functional Body, Metrics & Controls (`Inter`):** Renders figures, role achievements, audit bullet points, and analytical tables with maximum clarity and zero ambiguity.
- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** All numerical representations, balance figures, percentages, and financial deltas enforce tabular figure alignment for scan-friendly comparisons.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture implements a rigid 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content container width of 1200px, centering analytical balance and executive reading cadence.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (Single-column flow, edge margins of `1.25rem`, stacked KPI ribbons).
  - Tablet: `768px - 1024px` (6-column layout, two-column metric cards).
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12-column layout, multi-card dashboards, split executive biography and real-time metric panel).
- **Rhythm:** Multiples of 8px govern internal component architecture, maintaining structured horizontal and vertical scan lines across complex achievements.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy shadows, adopting crisp architectural layering, subtle tonal shifts, and micro-borders:

- **Base Layer:** Canvas surface (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient, whisper-light drop: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Active/Hover/Floating Filters):** White (`#FFFFFF`) with `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Subtle Layering:** Alternating data strips and metric backgrounds use `#F1F5F9` to create instant visual grouping without adding heavy borders.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, soft-cornered posture (`roundedness: 1`), balancing technical rigidity with contemporary interface refinement:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius. Maintains an authoritative, structured, and dossier-like feel.
- **Card Containers & Dashboard Modules:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius. Softens content perimeter while preserving straight lines.
- **Status Pills & Micro-Tags:** Fully rounded pill forms for specific categorical tags (e.g., "IFRS", "SOX Compliance", "ERP SAP S/4HANA") to differentiate qualitative credentials from rectangular quantitative metric blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Download Confidential Dossier / Board CV"):** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #0F172A`, 4px radius, 10px 20px padding. Hover: `#1E293B` with micro lift.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Financial Models / Case Studies"):** Background transparent, border `1px solid #0F172A`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Tertiary Accent (e.g., "Schedule Strategic Interview"):** Background `#0284C7`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#0369A1`.

### KPI Metric Badges & Dashboard Cards
- **Card Shell:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, 8px radius, 20px padding.
- **Top Row:** Uppercase micro-label (`label-sm`, color `#64748B`) paired with an amber delta chip (`#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text).
- **Core Value:** `kpi-metric` in `#0F172A` with tabular numeral spacing (e.g., `+$14.2M`, `34% Margin Expansion`).
- **Context Footer:** Compact footnote in `#475569` explaining benchmark scope.

### Career Timeline & Audit Trails
- **Structural Axis:** Solid `2px` left border in `#E2E8F0` anchoring past tenures.
- **Timeline Pin:** `10px` solid `#0284C7` node on active positions; hollow `#94A3B8` node on historical transitions.
- **Scope Headers:** Clear organizational division: Company, Title, Mandate, Budget Responsibility (P&L Scope), and Bulleted ROI Achievements.

### Chips & Competency Badges
- **Technical Competency Chips (e.g., "FP&A", "M&A Diligence", "Cash Flow Optimization"):** Background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`, `4px` radius, `4px 10px` padding.
- **Executive Endorsement Pill:** Amber-tinted background `#FFFBEB`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`, text `#92400E`.

### Form Fields & Inquiries
- **Input / Select Fields:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, `4px` radius. Focus ring: `2px` petrol blue (`#0284C7`) with zero blur offset.
